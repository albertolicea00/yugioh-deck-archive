# Code of Conduct

## Community standards

Everyone participating in this project deserves respectful treatment, regardless of experience, background, identity, disability, or personal circumstances. These standards apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces, and when representing the project elsewhere.

- Be patient, constructive, and considerate. Critique the work and explain disagreements with evidence.
- Welcome questions, acknowledge corrections, and respect different levels of technical and game knowledge.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and other contributors' time.
- Keep contributions relevant to the project.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone else's private information are unacceptable. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or helps address one is also unacceptable.

## Reporting

The maintainer is [@albertolicea00](https://github.com/albertolicea00). For a non-sensitive project concern, contact the maintainer in the relevant issue or PR. Do not post private information or confidential allegations publicly.

For abuse or a sensitive concern, use GitHub's **Report abuse** tools on the relevant content or account, or [contact GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact). This repository does not currently offer a separate confidential reporting channel. If the concern involves the maintainer, report it directly to GitHub.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock threads, and restrict participation. Responses will consider the severity, context, and recurrence of the behavior: a correction request or warning may be appropriate for a minor incident, while serious or repeated misconduct may lead to temporary or permanent exclusion.

People affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration in the relevant thread when appropriate, without disclosing private information or restarting harassment. Reports will be handled with discretion, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed on public GitHub surfaces.
